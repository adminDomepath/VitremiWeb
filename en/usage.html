VITREMI / USE

Intended use and limits

Vitremi helps organize eye breaks while using screens. You choose when to start, pause, adjust, or end a session.

Scope

Vitremi does not diagnose, treat, or prevent disease, and is not a substitute for advice from health professionals. Its records show elapsed time within a session; they do not prove a break was taken or measure actual screen use.

Reminder availability

Notifications, sounds, and Live Activities depend on permissions, settings, prepared coverage, and iOS behavior. We cannot guarantee their exact delivery time. Open the app periodically to refresh future reminders.
