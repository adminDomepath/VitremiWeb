VITREMI / USE

Intended use and limits

Vitremi helps you organize eye breaks while using screens. You choose when to start, pause, or end a session.

How to read your history

History shows the time that elapsed during the work and break phases of each session. A completed break means its timer finished. Vitremi does not measure how long you looked at a screen or check whether you looked away.

Reminder availability

Reminders, sounds, and Live Activities depend on your permissions, which reminders are prepared, and how iOS operates. Calendar shows how far ahead reminders are prepared. Open Vitremi periodically to refresh them.

Eye wellbeing

Vitremi is a tool for organizing breaks. It does not diagnose, treat, or prevent disease, and it does not replace care from a health professional.
