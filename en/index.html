Eye break reminders

When you spend a long time looking at a screen without breaks, you tend to blink less. Your eyes can feel dry, tired, or irritated, and your vision may blur temporarily. Vitremi reminds you when to look away.

See a break ↓
20:00MINUTES

Break

When the reminder appears, look away from the screen. Pick a fixed point about 20 feet (6 meters) away and keep your gaze there for 20 seconds. Looking into the distance gives your eyes a break from focusing up close and may ease eye strain.

Calendar

Schedule a session by choosing when it starts and which profile to use. Use the profile’s duration or set an end time yourself.

  • Once
  • Every day
  • Every week
  • Every month

With notifications enabled, scheduled reminders can reach you while Vitremi is closed.

Blink reminders

Turn on Blink Reminder in Settings and choose an interval from 1 to 30 minutes. Vitremi reminds you to blink during the work portion of a session, using your sound and Live Activity settings.

History

See how long each session lasted and how many breaks were recorded. Your history stays on your iPhone.
