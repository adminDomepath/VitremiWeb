VITREMI FOR IPHONE

Eye breaks for days spent at a screen.

Start a session on your iPhone. Vitremi reminds you when to look away, counts each break, and saves a summary when you're done.

How it works ↘

BREAK

A clear cue to look away.

When a break begins, Vitremi shifts to deep blue and shows its starry sky. The timer tells you when to return; the end reminder depends on your settings and iOS.

CALENDAR

Plan sessions when you need them.

Choose a day, a profile, and how long the session will last. Use a specific date or repeat the schedule.

Vitremi shows how far ahead reminders are prepared. Delivery depends on iOS permissions and limits.

HISTORY

Review earlier sessions.

When a session ends, you can review its duration and recorded breaks. This summarizes the routine you set up; it does not measure screen time.

Vitremi does not check whether you looked away. Your sessions and history stay on your iPhone, without an account.
