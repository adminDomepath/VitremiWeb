VITREMI / PRIVACY

Privacy policy

This policy describes the data used by Vitremi for iPhone. Last updated: November 15, 2026.

Data stored on your iPhone

Vitremi stores your profiles, preferences, schedules, sessions, and history locally. It requires no account, shows no ads, and includes no external analytics. It does not access the camera, location, microphone, or usage of other apps or screens. The app does not sync this data with iCloud; iPhone backups depend on your Apple settings.

Notifications and Live Activities

With your permission, Vitremi schedules local notifications and displays Live Activities using iOS services. Apple handles these features under its own policies.

If a build enables the optional remote Live Activity update service, the app sends that activity's push token, upcoming phase times, and the state needed to update it to a Vitremi server. The server uses this information to send updates through Apple Push Notification service; it does not receive your full history or stored profiles. Pending updates are replaced when the session changes. The server automatically removes inactive activities approximately eight hours after their last synchronization. Network requests may also create technical access metadata in hosting infrastructure.

Control and deletion

You can turn off notifications or Live Activities in iOS. “Delete all data” in Vitremi Settings removes local sessions, schedules, preferences, and history, and ends the current session. If the remote service is enabled and the app can reach it, Vitremi cancels pending updates when the activity ends. For questions or requests to delete technical service data, email admin@domepath.com.

This website

These pages are static and use no accounts, forms, Vitremi cookies, or Vitremi analytics. GitHub Pages, our hosting provider, may process technical access data under its privacy policy. If you email us, we use your message and address to respond to your request.

Contact and changes

Controller: DomePath. Contact: admin@domepath.com. If our data practices change, we will update this page and its date.
