VITREMI / PRIVACY

Privacy policy

This notice explains how DomePath, S.A. de C.V. handles data for Vitremi for iPhone and vitremi.com. Last updated: November 16, 2026.

Data stored on your iPhone

Vitremi stores the profiles, preferences, and schedules you set up, along with your sessions and history, on your iPhone. It uses this data to organize reminders and show your history. The app works without an account, ads, or external analytics. It does not access your camera, location, microphone, or usage of other apps. Vitremi does not sync this data with iCloud. iPhone backups depend on your iOS settings.

Notifications and Live Activities

Vitremi schedules local notifications with your permission. When you use a Live Activity, the app sends its push token, upcoming phase times, and the state needed to update it to a Vitremi server. The server uses this data to deliver updates through Apple Push Notification service (APNs). Your stored profiles and full history stay on your iPhone. See Apple’s privacy policy.

When the session changes, the server replaces pending updates. Inactive activities are automatically removed from the server about eight hours after their last synchronization. The hosting infrastructure may generate technical logs of network requests.

Control and deletion

You can withdraw notification permission or turn off Live Activities in iOS Settings. In Vitremi, Settings → Delete all data removes local data, including your preferences, schedules, sessions, and history, and ends the active session. When the activity ends, Vitremi requests cancellation of pending updates if it can reach the server.

This website and support emails

Vitremi.com works without accounts, forms, Vitremi cookies, or Vitremi analytics. GitHub Pages hosts the site and logs visitors’ IP addresses for security. See the GitHub Privacy Statement. If you email us, we use your address and the contents of your message to handle your request.

Privacy requests

Email leonardo.arias@domepath.com to request access, correction, deletion, or objection to the processing of your data, withdraw consent, or request limits on use or disclosure of your data. Tell us which right you wish to exercise and which data or request you want us to review. If we need to verify your identity, we will explain how.

We will publish changes to this notice on this page and update the date.
