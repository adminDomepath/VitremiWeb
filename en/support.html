VITREMI / SUPPORT

Vitremi support

For help, questions, or suggestions about Vitremi, email leonardo.arias@domepath.com.

If reminders do not arrive

  • In iPhone Settings, check that Vitremi has permission to send notifications. Check Focus, Scheduled Summary, and Silent mode if you expect to hear a reminder.
  • If a scheduled session does not appear, open Vitremi to refresh reminders and the Live Activity. Calendar shows how far ahead reminders are prepared.

What to include in your message

Include your iPhone model, iOS and Vitremi versions, what you expected to happen, and what steps you took. If you attach a screenshot, hide your personal information. Avoid sending passwords or access codes.

Data and privacy

To delete local data, open Vitremi and go to Settings → Delete all data. This also ends the active session. For other data or permission questions, learn how to make a privacy request.
